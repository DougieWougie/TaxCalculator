Mobile: the cockpit's weak spot

A fixed control rail has no room on a phone. Both options keep the rule "the number you're watching never leaves the screen while you fiddle" — they differ in where the controls go. Shown in the Instrument style you picked.

A

Controls in a drag-up sheet

Readout owns the screen. Controls live in a sheet docked at the bottom — it peeks with the control you last touched, and drags up to reveal the rest. You adjust with your thumb and watch the number above your hand.

B

I'd take A — it's the only one that preserves the thing you said you do, watching numbers move while you adjust, on a screen too small to show both otherwise. It costs a drag gesture to build and needs a non-gesture fallback (tap the sheet header to expand) for accessibility. B is safer and cheaper but on a phone you'll often be dragging a slider whose effect has scrolled off.
