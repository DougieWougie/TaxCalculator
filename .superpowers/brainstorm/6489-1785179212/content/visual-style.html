All three keep a light and a dark mode — what's shown is each one's primary mode. My pick: Instrument. It's the only one of the three that's genuinely uncommon for a tax calculator, and the cockpit layout you chose is already an instrument metaphor — monospaced tabular figures also stop numbers jittering sideways as they update while you drag a slider, which matters a lot when the whole point is watching them move.

Editorial is the safer distinctive choice and ages well. Tactile is the least risky but is closest to where you started, so it fixes "generic" the least.
